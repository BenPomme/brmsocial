# 04 — Email / contact coverage

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What share of Sant Cugat leads have a usable site email or site WhatsApp after scout+scrape, and what is the fallback for the rest (not Maps phone)?

## Answer

2 Sep 2026 after scout+scrape on 92 leads:

| Contact | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| Site | 68 | 74% |
| Email (site) | 45 | 49% |
| Maps phone (stored, not used to message) | 79 | 86% |
| WhatsApp on site | 6 | 7% |
| Reachable email or site WA | 46 | 50% |
| skip (no email, no site WA) | 46 | 50% |

Maps phone is in `leads.maps_phone` for the CRM. Outreach still does not call the Maps number. Gap: the 50% skip — later: more page guesses, Instagram, or a human list.
