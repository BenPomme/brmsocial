# Two products, two WhatsApp identities

BabyRock Social (Google review replies) and BabyRock Direct (the shop's own WhatsApp) are separate products. Social is what we sell now. Direct is coming later. They do not share a WhatsApp identity: Fil Babyrock is Babyrock talking to the Titulaire; Fil commerce is the shop's own number talking to its customers.

We considered folding the review-ask and the shop inbox into the 99 € Social subscription. That would mix two factories, two Meta setups, and two legal bases, and it would make Social unlaunchable until Meta Tech Provider works. We also considered a Babyrock-branded number that speaks to diners: the Titulaire would lose the thread. Rejected.

Code, Stripe SKUs, and the public site must name the product. Pay only accepts live Social SKUs. Direct has no SKU and no checkout.
