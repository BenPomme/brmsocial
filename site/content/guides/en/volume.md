## title
Why the number of reviews matters, not only the rating

## dek
A listing with 40 recent reviews and a 4.5 persuades more than a 4.9 with eight opinions from 2019. Google and customers look at both.

## impact_label
Estimated impact

## impact
In the SOCi study, **every 10 extra Google reviews went with +2.8 % listing conversion**. Replying to all reviews, versus none, went with **+16.4 %** of that conversion. These are large correlations. Do not add them together or write them into a contract.

## body
The rating moves slowly. The count and the date of the last review are visible in a second. An independent lives on that more than a chain does.

You do not control who writes. You control whether you reply, and whether you ask fairly (QR, same sentence for everyone).

More reviews do not replace a better plate. They do stop a single 2-star from 2024 being the only recent thing on the listing.

BabyRock Social handles the replies. Volume comes from people who visited and from the ask you make. Those are not the same product.
