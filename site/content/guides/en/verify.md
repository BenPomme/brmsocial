## title
How to verify your Google Business Profile

## dek
If there is no Reply button on your reviews, the listing is probably not verified, or you are signed in with the wrong Google account. Verification is how you take ownership of the profile and start answering.

## impact_label
Why it matters

## impact
Google will not let you edit hours, photos, or replies until the profile is verified. That is the whole point of the step. Without it, customers can still write. You cannot answer.

## body
Search Google for your business name and city, while signed in. If the listing exists and says Claim this business, claim it. If it does not exist, add it. Then choose Get verified. Google picks the method. You cannot switch it for fun.

The usual options are a phone call or SMS to the business number, an email, a live video call from the shop, or a postcard with a code. Video is often the fastest when it is offered: you stand at the door, show the sign, the street, and something only the owner would have (till, keys, kitchen). Postcard can take up to two weeks. Do not change the name or address while the card is in the post, or the code dies.

After you send the proof, Google can take up to five business days. Sometimes it is instant if the website is already in Search Console.

If the Reply button is still missing: check you are in the same Google account that owns the listing. A staff Gmail is not enough if the profile sits on another login. Ask the owner to add you as a manager, not as a second owner, from Business Profile settings, People and access.

If Get verified appears again after you thought you were done, Google did not finish. Try the next method they show. Do not create a second listing to “start clean”. Duplicates cause more pain than they save.

Once you are verified, you can reply. That is the moment the other guides on this site start to work.
