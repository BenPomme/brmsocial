# Review-volume agencies and competitor features vs Social + Direct

**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Scope:** Do Spanish “más reseñas / mes” agencies fabricate Google reviews? Which features they sell that BabyRock Social does not, and which Social + Direct jobs they typically will not do.  
**Method:** Vendor pages the company controls, Google Maps UGC policy, BOE (LCD + TRLGDCU as amended). Blog roundups and Capterra are not used as price sources.

This note does not change `01-produit.md`. Social still does not promise more avis. Direct still asks after a known visit, with no gating.

---

## Verdict in brief

They sell **commissioned Google reviews as a quota**. Google treats paid reviews as **Fake Engagement** even when a human types them. Spanish unfair-competition law treats **commissioning** fake or distorted consumer reviews as an always-unfair practice. We cannot, from outside, prove that every Mundo Reseñas “reseñador” never visited the shop. We also do not need that proof: **payment for the avis is enough** under Google’s rule, and briefing writers on keywords / 5★ packs is enough to call the SKU what it is.

Mundo Reseñas’ Spain homepage says “Reseñas gestionadas · No compradas” while the same site sells **12–90 reseñas/mes** from a “red propia de reseñadores” and a **tienda** of 5–100 one-shot packs titled “comprar”. Their own legal blog says buying reviews in Spain is illegal. That is the product, not a rumour.

## 1. Spectrum (use these words, not “fake” as a single bucket)

| Kind | What it is | Google | ES law (short) | Mundo Reseñas / peers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Bot / stolen identity** | Never a person, or a stolen Maps account | Fake engagement | LCD 27.8 commissioning | They **deny** this; they attack “agencias de humo” that use bots |
| **B. Paid reviewer network** | Humans paid (or otherwise compensated) to leave a Google avis, often to a monthly quota | **Paid reviews are Fake Engagement**, whether or not someone walked in | LCD 27.8 “encarguen … reseñas o aprobaciones de consumidores falsas”; TRLGDCU 20.4 on presenting reviews as from real users | **This is the SKU.** “Red propia”, packs, “reseñador fijo”, reposición if Google deletes |
| **C. Incentives** | Discount / gift for an avis | Forbidden | Same family (undeclared consideration) | Not their headline offer |
| **D. Gating** | Only “satisfied” / 4–5★ customers get the Google link | Selective solicitation forbidden | Distortion of the public set of reviews | Partoo Review Booster copy: SMS to **clientes satisfechos**. Reply Champion used to sell a “smart review gate”; current blog says gating is risky |
| **E. Legal ask** | Same Google link to every eligible customer after a real visit, no pay, no star filter | Allowed | TRLGDCU 20.4 wants a real purchase/use; Ley 10/2025 tightened how businesses that **host** reviews must treat them | Direct’s planned job. Mundo also sells a “Motor Conseguir reseñas” QR as a side tool — that part can be legal; the quota is not |

Google, primary: [Prohibited & restricted content](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114) and the Fake Engagement line: “Reviews or ratings that have been **paid for, directly or in kind**.” Merchants may not “offer incentives … in exchange for posting any review” and may not “selectively solicit positive reviews.”

Spain, primary:

- **LCD art. 27.7–27.8** (Ley 3/1991, as amended by RDL 24/2021, Omnibus): always-unfair to claim reviews are from real purchasers without reasonable checks, and to **add or commission** another person to include fake consumer reviews or to distort reviews. [BOE consolidado](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/1991/01/10/3/con)
- **TRLGDCU art. 20.4** as amended by **Ley 10/2025** (BOE-A-2025-26698): a business that gives access to consumer reviews must say whether it guarantees they come from people who actually used the good/service; reviews so presented must refer to use in the **30 calendar days** before the review; the business may ask for deletion if it can prove the author never used it. [TRLGDCU consolidado](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rdlg/2007/11/16/1/con)

Ley 10/2025 is a customer-service law that **amends** TRLGDCU 20.4. Mundo’s blog (“sanciones 150 €–1.000.000 €, cierre 5 años”) is **their** reading of the penalty schedule, not a substitute for the BOE. Do not quote Mundo’s euro bands as law.

## 4. Killer features they sell that we do not (Social today)

Ranked by how often a titulaire will hear them in a WhatsApp close.

1. **A promised number of new Google avis per month** (12 / 25 / 50 / 90, or a 5–100 pack). We refuse this as a SKU.
2. **Geo-grid ranking map** (49 points, “sales el #2 en tu zona”). We do not sell Maps rank.
3. **Competitor radar** (their rating, pace, new photos vs yours).
4. **Listing shield** (alert if Google or a stranger edits hours/category).
5. **GBP hygiene as a product** (categories, photos, services) — we use the fiche for replies; we do not sell “ficha impecable” as the offer.
6. **Website widget of stars / last positives.**
7. **SMS or email review-request campaigns** from a CSV / caisse list (Reply Champion, Partoo Booster, NiceJob).
8. **Owner dashboard** (approve AI replies in one click, pause plan, download PDF). Our titulaire stays on **Fil Babyrock**.
9. **Multi-platform inbox** (Facebook, Tripadvisor with Google) — Partoo.
10. **Autopilot publish** with no human (RepliFast Pro). We keep an **opérateur** click; 1–3★ wait for the titulaire.
11. **Zone exclusivity** (“we will not take the shop across the street”).
12. **Gating / “only satisfied customers”** as a growth lever.

Items 2–6 are **legal** product. They are still not Social. Some could become a Monday recap line later (`adjacent-services.md`); they are not a reason to change the 99 € promise.

## 6. Open uncertainties

- We have not sat in Mundo’s reviewer Slack. Visit-or-not for each avis is unproven. The **paid quota** is proven on their own pages.
- `/argentina/` live-redirected to the ES home on 4 Sep 2026; the “Comprar Reseñas Google Argentina” HTML is still in the public index.
- Reply Champion’s live pricing page no longer pushes “smart review gate” as hard as the April 2026 generation URL; treat gating as a **category habit**, confirm copy before naming them in a sales fight.
- Ley 10/2025’s 30-day rule in TRLGDCU 20.4 is written for businesses that **facilitate access to reviews they publish**. How Consumo applies that to **Google Maps** (Google is the publisher) is not a closed administrative practice we have on file. Google’s own paid-review ban does not depend on that debate.
- Stamina Marketing should be dropped from the colleague’s four-segment table.

## 7. What this means for BabyRock

Do not compete with Mundo on “12 reseñas este mes.” That fight is illegal under Google’s rule and is the thing their own legal blog tells the shop not to buy.

When a titulaire says “pero ellos me ponen reseñas,” the accurate line is: they sell **avis escritos por una red pagada**; Google can strip them; we reply to the avis that real clients already left; later Direct can ask **that same client**, after a visit we actually booked, without a star filter.
